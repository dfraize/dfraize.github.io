Mercer - FSM Pro
I led UI design and development on FSM Pro, rebuilding an aging native iOS app as a modern web application, a redesign that launched on time, on budget, and was well received.

The Challenge

FSM Pro existed only as a native iOS app, limiting access and giving the product a dated feel compared to competitors.

My Role

Lead UI Designer & Developer

Project Team

1 UX Designer, 1 UI Designer

Duration

4 Months

Project Status

Went live into production

Design Methodology

I led the redesign of a product that originally existed as a native iOS app, using Highcharts.js to rebuild its charting for the web.

Business Goals

Redesign the interface and workflows to help the product compete in market, bringing a more modern look and feel.

Desktop Views

The web redesign was well received and opened the product up to a much wider audience than the native app ever could.
