Fidelity - Design System
I led the creation of FXD, Fidelity Institutional's design system, built in Figma, establishing color palettes, themes, typography, iconography, grids, and a documented component library that gave designers and developers a shared, centralized source of truth.

The Challenge

Design work at Fidelity Institutional was happening in disconnected tools and formats, making it hard to keep designers and developers aligned on a consistent product experience.

My Role

UX/UI Designer (lead)

Project Team

2 UX Designers, 2 Front-end Developers

Duration

Ongoing

Project Status

Continuous deployment

Design Methodology

I combined brand guidelines with UX principles to build a robust design system within Figma, centralizing all design work into one collaborative environment for easier updates and shared workflows. I also contributed to UX discovery, ideation, and specification phases, and documented every component in detail for developers.

Business Goals

Set up an environment where business analysts, consultants, UX designers, UI designers, and developers could centralize on product design decisions, backed by a codebase that matched what was in the design system.

Desktop Views

FXD is still growing today, giving Fidelity Institutional's design and development teams a shared foundation to build from instead of reinventing patterns project by project.
