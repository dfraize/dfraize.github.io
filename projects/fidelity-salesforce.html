Fidelity - Salesforce
I led the UX design effort to modernize Fidelity's internal Salesforce implementation, cleaning up outdated patterns and giving users clearer access to financial best-practice guidance. The work was well received across the team.

The Challenge

The existing Salesforce implementation had grown cluttered and outdated over time, making it harder for users to find and act on financial guidance.

My Role

UX Design

Project Team

1 UX Designer, Development team

Duration

Ongoing

Project Status

Continuous deployment

Design Methodology

I worked with stakeholders to collect requirements, iterated through design reviews, and once we had a v1, brought in the development team for joint review sessions.

Business Goals

Clean up the overall Salesforce implementation and begin modernizing the design.

Desktop Views

The modernized experience was well received, and it's set a cleaner foundation for future Salesforce work at Fidelity.
