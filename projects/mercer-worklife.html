Mercer - Worklife
As UI Developer on Mercer Worklife, I helped bring a new pension management product to market, centralizing government filings, investments, and plan management in one place, with a white-label option. The product launched on time, on budget, and was well received.

The Challenge

Users had no single place to view their government filings, investments, and pension details, a fragmented experience that made plan management harder than it needed to be.

My Role

UI Developer

Project Team

1 UX Designer, 1 Visual Designer, 2 UI Developers

Duration

5 Months

Project Status

Went live into production

Design Methodology

I worked with stakeholders to gather requirements, iterated through design reviews, then brought in the development team for review sessions once a v1 was ready, incorporating their feedback into the final designs.

Business Goals

Bring a new product to market for easier pension plan analysis, centralizing everything for a more holistic view and management of a user's plan.

Desktop Views

Worklife launched on time and on budget, giving users a genuinely simpler way to manage their pension in one place.
