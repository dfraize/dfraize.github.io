Fidelity - Project Spark
I led the UX design for Project Spark, a new Fidelity product giving users a single place to analyze their pension plan, delivered on time and on budget, and well received from launch.

The Challenge

Pension plan information was scattered across disconnected tools, making it hard for users to get a clear, holistic view of their retirement finances in one place.

My Role

UX Design

Project Team

1 UX Designer, Outsourced for build

Duration

1 Month

Project Status

Went live into production

Design Methodology

I worked with stakeholders to gather requirements, then moved through design iterations and review cycles. Once we had a v1, I brought in the development team for review sessions and incorporated their feedback into the final designs.

Business Goals

Bring a new product to market for easier pension plan analysis, centralizing everything into one place for a more holistic view of personal money management.

Desktop Views

Spark shipped on time and on budget, a reminder that even small, focused products can meaningfully simplify a complex decision for users.
