Fidelity - Tableau Dashboard
I set the design direction for Fidelity's internal Tableau dashboards, replacing a patchwork of one-off builds with a more consistent, modern approach. The work was well received internally.

The Challenge

Dashboards had been built independently by different teams based on individual requests, resulting in an inconsistent and dated experience for internal users.

My Role

UX Design

Project Team

1 UX Designer, Development team

Duration

Ongoing

Project Status

Continuous deployment

Design Methodology

I worked with stakeholders to collect requirements, iterated through design reviews, then partnered with the development team on implementation reviews once a v1 was ready.

Business Goals

Clean up the overall implementation of Tableau dashboards and begin modernizing the design.

Desktop Views

The refreshed direction gave internal teams a clearer, more consistent way to work with their data.
