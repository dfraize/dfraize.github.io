# Portfolio Site — Project Notes

## Global copy style rules

These apply to **all text on the site** — project pages, homepage, testimonials, resume, anywhere — not just new project pages.

- **No em dashes.** Use a period, comma, or colon instead. Em dashes had crept into nearly every page's copy and read as a repetitive tic once there were a dozen pages of it. When adding or editing any copy (including data files like `testimonials-data.json` and `projects-data.json` that generate markup), check for and remove em dashes before considering the work done.
- Confident, not corporate — avoid buzzword-stacking ("synergized," "leveraged," overused "spearheaded").
- Concise — short paragraphs, no filler, every sentence earns its place.

Note: en dashes (`–`) used in date ranges (e.g. "2021–2025") are a different, intentional convention and are not affected by the em-dash rule above.
