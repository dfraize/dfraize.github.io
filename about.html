The Designer Who Speaks Developer

What started as a simple HTML course in 1999 accidentally launched a 25-year journey at the intersection of design and code. I discovered something rare: I love solving user problems through design and I have a knack for understanding how to build those solutions at scale.

This unique perspective has made me a "bridge-builder" someone who can envision beautiful, user-centered experiences while architecting the design systems that make them sustainable. At FM Global, I pioneered a comprehensive design system that achieved 85% adoption across 300+ developers and reduced UI development time by 40%. But the real win? Consistent, accessible experiences that actually serve user needs.

Now as a UX Designer at Fidelity, I'm focused on what I've always been passionate about: creating intuitive experiences that help people navigate complex decisions with confidence. Whether I'm designing for institutional clients or reimagining internal tools, I bring systematic thinking and deep technical fluency to every user challenge.
