The Designer Who Speaks Developer

I started my career working with HTML in the late 1990s, which naturally led me into interface design and front-end development. Over time, I found myself drawn more toward solving user problems than simply implementing solutions understanding why something should work a certain way became just as important as how it was built.

That path shaped the way I work today. I approach design with a strong systems mindset, balancing user needs, business goals, and technical realities. I’m comfortable collaborating closely with product managers and engineers, and I often act as a bridge between disciplines to help teams move forward with clarity and alignment.

At FM Global, I played a key role in establishing and evolving a shared design system, helping teams deliver more consistent, accessible, and efficient user experiences across products.

Currently at Fidelity Investments, I focus on designing intuitive experiences for complex products and internal tools. My work emphasizes clarity, usability, and scalability ensuring that users can confidently navigate systems that are inherently complicated.
